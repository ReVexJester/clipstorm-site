ClipStorm Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Who uses the app

ClipStorm Uploader is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @clipstorm.zone, to upload that account's own videos as drafts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information from TikTok (open ID and display name). The app uses it only to confirm that the authorized account is @clipstorm.zone.
  • The OAuth access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, so the owner can upload drafts.
  • The video files the owner chooses to upload. They are sent directly from the owner's computer to TikTok.

Storage

Tokens are kept in a local file on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not store data anywhere else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data goes only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions) or with the app's revoke command, which also deletes the local token file. After revocation, no data remains in the app.
