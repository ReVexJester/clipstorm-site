ClipStorm Uploader

ClipStorm Uploader is a small desktop tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @clipstorm.zone. It sends finished short gaming clips from the owner's computer to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner then reviews, captions and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
